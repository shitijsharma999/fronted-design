THE STORY

Six feet of frontage.
A lifetime of departures.

In 1983, Mishraji bought this stall with ₹18,000 borrowed from his brother. The railway gave him six feet of platform frontage.

He filled all six.

Over the years, the little space became a home for students, dreamers, office-goers and lost travellers. It has seen love stories, exam results, friendships and goodbyes.

Now, after 43 years, Mishraji is retiring. The stall will close on 19 October 2026.

“Books are the only thing you can buy that makes you richer.”
— Mishraji
